  Founded in Naples in the early 1900s, Rescigno is a company specialising in fresh bread and baked goods. Over time, alongside the historic bakery on Via Cirillo 74 in Naples, new ventures have joined the Rescigno family — including the new restaurant @rescignoristorante.

  For the restaurant we created video and photographic content designed to express the Neapolitan soul, with a touch of jazz and a vintage mood in harmony with the atmosphere and philosophy of the place: to be a family living room.

  "Impastando un Sogno" was the first step — the commercial produced for the opening. From the workshop of Antonio Rescigno's historic bakery, the story moves toward the restaurant, alongside Maria, his wife and lifelong partner, toward the new dream.
