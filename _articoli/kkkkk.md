intro: >-
  Hanging a photograph you have taken is one of the hardest

  things in the world.


  Not only because you practically need a degree in “hammering nails,” but because once it is there,

  on the wall, that photograph you may have taken purely on instinct or simply for the pleasure of

  capturing a moment is no longer yours alone. It belongs to everyone - for real.

  Not only in the digital world, but in the real one. In the world of real gazes. They interpret. They

  analyze.


  And you realize that you have created something.

  And the “something” we are telling you about is "The City That Looks", by Alessandra Mustilli, held

  in Naples in October 2025.
